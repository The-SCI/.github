# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers of **The SCI SolCielo Innovacion Private Limited™** (`github.com/The-SCI`) pledge to make participation in our repositories and communities a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, experience level, or identity.

## Expected Behavior

- Communicate constructively, respectfully, and concisely in issues, pull requests, and discussions.
- Focus feedback on technical architecture, reproducibility, and user impact.
- Respect confidentiality and responsible security disclosure guidelines.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or unacceptable behavior may be reported to the core team at `support@thesci.co.in`. All complaints will be reviewed promptly and fairly.
